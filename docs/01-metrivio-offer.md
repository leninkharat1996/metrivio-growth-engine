# Metrivio Offer Document

**Purpose of this document:** Reference material for the freelance developer building the Metrivio LinkedIn Prospecting Skill, and for the Skill itself to understand what Metrivio sells, why it matters, and how to talk about it without overstating anything.

**Status:** This document contains only confirmed positioning and offer mechanics as defined by Metrivio's founder. It contains no client results, because none exist yet.

---

## 1. What Metrivio Is

Metrivio is an independent Marketing Efficiency Diagnostic for DTC/ecommerce brands. It is a paid analysis engagement, not a subscription, not a dashboard, and not a media-buying service.

Metrivio's job is to look across a brand's paid acquisition activity (Meta, Google, Amazon where relevant), funnel/conversion performance, and customer economics, and turn that data into a clear set of decisions: what to **stop**, **fix**, **scale**, **test**, and **reallocate**.

The core differentiator is **independence**. Metrivio is paid to evaluate whether marketing spend is allocated correctly — it is not paid to manage that spend, and it has no financial incentive to recommend more spend, more channels, or more campaigns. This is structurally different from an agency, which is typically compensated in a way that is at least partially tied to media spend or scope of work under management.

## 2. Who It Is For

Primary buyer: the founder, co-founder, CEO, or senior marketing decision-maker at a DTC/ecommerce brand doing roughly $1M–$10M in annual revenue, spending meaningfully on paid acquisition (Metrivio's preferred threshold is $15k+/month), with active Meta and/or Google activity and enough operating history to have real data to analyze.

This is a founder-led or founder-influenced buying decision. The person Metrivio talks to needs to be someone who can authorize a $997 spend and who is close enough to the marketing numbers to feel the cost of misallocated budget.

## 3. The Problem It Solves

At $1M–$10M in revenue with $15k+/month in paid acquisition, most founders are making stop/scale/reallocate decisions on marketing spend without an independent, structured, cross-channel view of where the money is actually working. Reporting tends to be channel-siloed (Meta dashboard, Google dashboard, Shopify analytics) rather than integrated into a single set of prioritized decisions. Agencies and internal teams, however competent, are rarely incentivized or resourced to conduct the kind of skeptical, cross-channel review that questions the current allocation itself.

The result: budget continues flowing into campaigns, channels, or tactics that are underperforming, simply because no one with the right independence and the right structured process has stopped to check.

## 4. Why the Problem Matters

At this revenue and spend range, a misallocated marketing budget has real financial consequences — every dollar spent on an underperforming channel or campaign is a dollar not available for something that would have worked better, and at $15k+/month sustained over time, the compounding cost of a wrong allocation is material to the business.

Metrivio's promise is built around this consequence, not around a guaranteed dollar figure: *"Find the marketing spend you should stop, fix, and reallocate before you waste another $10k."* This is a framing of financial stakes, not a commitment that Metrivio will save any specific client $10,000. No savings, ROAS improvement, or revenue outcome should ever be promised or implied as guaranteed.

## 5. What the Marketing Efficiency Diagnostic Looks At

The diagnostic analyzes whatever data is available and accessible across:

- **Meta** — campaign, ad set, and creative-level performance
- **Google** — Search/Shopping/Performance Max campaign performance
- **Amazon** — where the brand has meaningful Amazon activity
- **Shopify** — funnel, conversion, and site performance data
- **Funnel/conversion performance** — landing page, checkout, and conversion-path data
- **Customer economics** — CAC, repeat purchase behavior, and related metrics, where data permits

Where a data source is unavailable or access isn't granted, the diagnostic states that limitation plainly rather than filling the gap with assumption or estimation presented as fact.

## 6. What the Client Receives

- Campaign-level analysis across available channels
- Cross-channel analysis (how channels are performing relative to each other, not just individually)
- Funnel/conversion analysis
- Customer economics analysis, where data permits
- Budget allocation analysis
- Prioritized recommendations
- A 30-day action plan
- A findings walkthrough (live or recorded)
- A concise action sheet

The deliverable is not "a long report." It is a small number of clear, prioritized decisions the founder can act on immediately.

## 7. The STOP / FIX / SCALE / TEST / REALLOCATE Framework

Every finding in the diagnostic is classified into one of five categories:

- **STOP** — spend that should potentially be stopped, or investigated for stopping
- **FIX** — spend or setup where a problem appears to exist and needs investigation or repair
- **SCALE** — spend where the evidence supports investigating increased investment
- **TEST** — experiments that deserve priority given the current data
- **REALLOCATE** — where budget should potentially move, and the reasoning behind it

This framework is the organizing structure of the entire deliverable — the sample report, the website, and the sales conversation should all use this same language.

## 8. The Diagnostic Delivery Process

1. Paid diagnostic engagement confirmed ($997 for the first five founding clients)
2. Data collection — client provides access/exports across relevant channels
3. Analysis (target: 5 business days from receipt of required data)
4. Findings delivered — walkthrough plus action sheet
5. Optional 14-day implementation sprint offered where appropriate

If client-provided data is delayed or incomplete, the 5-day delivery clock is understood to start from the point complete data is received, not from the point of purchase. There is no complicated SLA or per-day penalty structure attached to this.

## 9. The 15-Minute Fit Call

The fit call is a qualification and positioning conversation, not a free mini-audit. Its purpose:

- Confirm there's a real, current marketing allocation problem
- Understand current acquisition spend and decision-making process
- Establish the financial consequence of the status quo
- Position the diagnostic as the next step
- Handle objections
- Close on payment and schedule data collection

No free analysis, audit, or strategy advice is given on this call. The value exchange is: Metrivio invests 15 minutes to determine fit; the prospect invests 15 minutes to determine whether the diagnostic is worth $997 to them.

## 10. What Happens After the Diagnostic

The client receives the findings walkthrough and action sheet. From there, three paths are possible:

1. The client implements the recommendations themselves or via their existing team/agency
2. The client engages Metrivio for the optional 14-day implementation sprint
3. The client requests ongoing consulting (evaluated case by case, not a standard offer at this stage)

## 11. Optional Implementation Support (14-Day Sprint)

After the diagnostic, Metrivio may offer a scoped 14-day implementation sprint covering work such as: implementing agreed recommendations, measurement fixes, campaign/budget changes, test setup, interpreting early results, and decision support.

This is not unlimited consulting and is not given away for free. Scope and pricing are defined around actual delivery economics at the time the offer is formalized — there is no arbitrary value number attached to it in this document.

## 12. Why Metrivio Is Different From an Agency

An agency is typically paid to execute and manage media spend, which creates a structural incentive to keep managing (and often growing) that spend. Metrivio is paid once, for an independent diagnostic, with no ongoing media management relationship required. This means Metrivio has no financial reason to tell a client to keep spending on an underperforming channel, add more channels, or avoid recommending a stop.

## 13. Why an Independent Diagnostic Is Valuable

Even brands with a competent agency or internal team benefit from a second, independent, structured look — specifically because the people currently managing the spend are rarely positioned (structurally or incentive-wise) to recommend stopping or reallocating away from their own work. Metrivio's value is precisely that it has no stake in the current allocation.

## 14. What Type of Company Gets the Most Value

- $1M–$10M annual revenue DTC/ecommerce brand
- $15k+/month in paid acquisition, ideally across more than one channel
- Founder or senior marketing leader who owns budget allocation decisions
- Enough operating history and data volume for patterns to be analyzable
- Currently making allocation decisions without a structured, independent, cross-channel review

## 15. What Type of Company Does NOT Need Metrivio

- Pre-revenue or very early-stage brands with minimal paid acquisition spend or data history
- Brands spending meaningfully below the $15k/month threshold, where the diagnostic's cost-to-value ratio weakens
- Brands that are not DTC/ecommerce (no meaningful direct paid acquisition motion to analyze)
- Brands already receiving a genuinely independent, structured, cross-channel allocation review from a source with no incentive to keep spend where it is
- Companies where the decision-maker on the call has no real authority over marketing budget allocation

### Notes for the Skill

- Never claim a specific prospect's marketing is "wasted," "underperforming," or "misallocated" without evidence — the diagnostic *investigates* this, it does not presume it.
- Never state or imply Metrivio has existing clients, case studies, or results unless explicitly instructed otherwise (see Document 3).
- $997 is the founding-client price for the first five clients only. Do not treat it as a permanent price point in any generated content.
