# Metrivio Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) — Detailed Reference for the Prospecting Skill

**Purpose:** This document tells the Skill *who to prioritize* and *why*, and *how to score them*. It is designed to produce precision over volume. A prospect that is merely "a DTC brand" is not a qualified prospect — a prospect where Metrivio's problem is plausibly relevant *right now* is.

**Core rule for the Skill:** Technology signals are supporting evidence only. Technology stack alone (e.g., "uses Shopify + Klaviyo") must never be sufficient to qualify or score a prospect highly. Revenue and ad spend must never be invented — every claim about a prospect must be tagged with an evidence tier (see Section 4).

---

## 1. Ideal Person

- Founder, Co-founder, CEO, or Owner of the brand, **or**
- Senior marketing decision-maker (VP Marketing, Head of Growth, Director of Marketing) **with confirmed authority over budget allocation**, at a founder-led company where that person is empowered to make and act on spend decisions without lengthy internal approval chains

A person is NOT the right target if their role suggests execution without allocation authority (e.g., "Paid Social Specialist," "Marketing Coordinator," "Ecommerce Assistant") — these people cannot authorize a $997 purchase decision independently and are unlikely to be the buyer.

## 2. Ideal Company

- DTC and/or ecommerce brand (owns the customer relationship directly; not a pure B2B wholesaler or pure marketplace-only reseller with no owned acquisition)
- Meaningful paid acquisition motion — the brand actively spends to acquire customers, not purely organic/referral-driven
- Operating history sufficient to have real performance data (a brand launched last month has nothing to diagnose yet)

## 3. Revenue Range

**Target: approximately $1M–$10M annual revenue.**

This is a *preference range*, not a hard cutoff enforced by fabricated numbers. The Skill should never assert a specific revenue figure unless it is confirmed or has strong public evidence (e.g., a founder-stated figure in an interview, a funding announcement with disclosed revenue). Absent that, revenue should be scored as a *plausibility band* based on indirect signals (team size, ad spend signals, store maturity, press coverage) and tagged accordingly under the evidence system in Section 4.

## 4. Evidence Tiers (applies to every factual claim about a prospect)

Every claim the Skill makes about revenue, ad spend, team size, or company scale must be tagged:

- **CONFIRMED** — stated directly by the company/founder in a primary source (company site, founder interview, official press release, filing)
- **STRONG EVIDENCE** — multiple independent, credible indirect signals converge on the same conclusion (e.g., job postings + ad library activity + store scale signals all point the same direction)
- **LIKELY** — a single reasonable indirect signal, not corroborated
- **UNKNOWN** — no usable signal; the Skill must not guess

A prospect record with mostly UNKNOWN tags on core qualifying criteria (revenue, spend) should be scored conservatively — see Section 8.

## 5. Paid Acquisition Requirements

**Preferred: $15k+/month in paid acquisition spend.**

Direct spend figures are almost never publicly available, so the Skill should rely on indirect but legitimate signals:

- Active, sustained presence in Meta Ad Library (multiple concurrent ads, run over weeks/months, not a single test ad)
- Visible Google Search/Shopping ad presence for branded and non-branded terms
- Job postings indicating a paid media function (in-house media buyer, growth marketer) — suggests spend large enough to justify a dedicated role
- Agency case studies or public agency client lists naming the brand (if the brand appears as a named client of a paid media agency)

A brand with only sporadic, low-volume, or single-channel ad activity should be scored down — this doesn't meet the "enough data and complexity to diagnose" bar.

## 6. Marketing Maturity

Positive signals:
- Multiple concurrent paid channels (not just one)
- Evidence of structured campaigns (multiple ad variants, ongoing creative refresh, retargeting presence)
- Existing analytics/tagging infrastructure (GA4, Meta Pixel, GTM) — again, supporting evidence only

Negative signals:
- Single, static ad running for a long period with no variation (may indicate low sophistication or low spend)
- No visible tracking infrastructure at all

## 7. Ecommerce Maturity

Positive signals:
- Shopify or Shopify Plus storefront (Plus specifically suggests higher revenue tier, but is still supporting evidence, not proof)
- Established post-purchase/retention stack (Klaviyo, Recharge, Yotpo, Gorgias) suggesting the business has moved past early-stage
- Multiple product lines / SKUs, indicating an established catalog rather than a single-product startup

Negative signals:
- Single-product pre-launch or just-launched store
- No functioning checkout, or store clearly in beta/test state

## 8. Founder Involvement

Best-fit prospects have visible founder presence in the business's public communication (founder actively posts on LinkedIn about the business, is quoted in press, appears in brand content). This correlates with founder-influenced decision-making, which is the buying dynamic Metrivio depends on. Absence of this signal is not disqualifying but reduces confidence that the person the Skill would reach is the actual decision-maker.

## 9. Company Size Signals

Reasonable indirect signals (all supporting evidence, none individually confirmatory):
- LinkedIn company page employee count (very rough proxy)
- Number and seniority of marketing-related job postings
- Warehouse/fulfillment job postings (suggests physical scale of operations)

## 10. Geography Considerations

No hard geographic restriction is defined in current strategy. Note this as an open question for validation (see final review). In absence of guidance, the Skill should not exclude prospects on geography alone, but should flag geography as a data point (time zone relevance for calls, English-language market for outbound, applicable email/marketing law jurisdiction).

## 11. Business Model

- DTC (direct-to-consumer, brand sells directly via its own site and/or Amazon) is the target
- Marketplace-only sellers with no owned funnel/acquisition (pure Amazon FBA arbitrage, no brand site) are a weaker fit — there's less cross-channel complexity to diagnose
- Pure B2B or wholesale-only businesses are out of scope

## 12. Technology Signals (Supporting Evidence Only)

Relevant stack signals: Shopify, Shopify Plus, Klaviyo, Gorgias, Recharge, Yotpo, Meta Pixel, GA4, Google Tag Manager, and comparable ecommerce/marketing tools.

**Reiterating the core rule: tech stack alone never qualifies a prospect.** It contributes to the "marketing maturity" and "ecommerce maturity" signals above, nothing more.

## 13. Positive Signals (Summary)

- Confirmed or strong-evidence revenue in $1M–$10M range
- Confirmed or strong-evidence paid spend at or above $15k/month
- Multiple active paid channels
- Founder visibly involved in business decisions/communication
- Established ecommerce/retention tech stack
- Recent buying-trigger event (see Section 15)

## 14. Negative Signals (Summary)

- No visible paid acquisition activity
- Single-product, pre-revenue, or clearly early-stage store
- Marketing function appears to be a single junior generalist with no evidence of budget authority
- No founder or senior-decision-maker visibility at all

## 15. Exclusion Criteria (Automatic Reject Regardless of Other Signals)

- Not DTC/ecommerce (pure B2B, pure service business, pure wholesale)
- No detectable paid acquisition activity of any kind
- Company appears defunct, store non-functional, or no activity in the last 6+ months
- Only contactable person found has no plausible budget authority (e.g., only a customer service or fulfillment contact is discoverable)

## 16. Buying / Trigger Signals ("Why Now")

Legitimate, observable signals that suggest the allocation problem may matter *right now* — never invented:

- New product launch
- Store or brand expansion (new geography, new marketplace)
- Active hiring for marketing/growth roles (suggests scaling spend, or a gap the diagnostic could fill)
- Visible increase in ad volume/variants recently
- New paid channel recently added (e.g., just started Google ads, previously Meta-only)
- Funding or growth announcement (press, LinkedIn post)
- Visible funnel/offer changes (new landing pages, new promotional structure)

Every "Why Now" reason surfaced by the Skill must cite the specific observable evidence and its source URL. No signal, no "why now" claim.

## 17. Signs of Marketing Complexity (Good Fit Indicator)

- Multiple concurrent channels
- Multiple campaign types (prospecting + retargeting)
- Evidence of experimentation (creative variety, landing page variants)

## 18. Signs the Company May Need Better Budget Allocation

- Long-running ad creative with no apparent refresh (stagnation signal)
- Presence across many channels without an obvious focus (possible spread-too-thin signal)
- Recent addition of a new channel without clear resulting change in overall ad volume patterns (possible sign of shifting spend without a structured framework)

These are **hypotheses the diagnostic would investigate**, not conclusions the Skill should assert as fact in outreach copy.

## 19. Signs the Company Is Too Small

- No visible paid acquisition, or only minimal/test-level ad activity
- Single-product, early-stage store with no retention infrastructure
- Team size signals suggesting a solo-founder or 1–2 person operation with no dedicated marketing function

## 20. Signs the Company Is Too Large

- Enterprise-scale, multi-brand portfolio companies or large public retailers — Metrivio's diagnostic model and $997 founding price point are built for a leaner, founder-led buying process, not enterprise procurement
- Evidence of an internal, sophisticated marketing analytics/data science function already doing this kind of cross-channel work at scale (reduces need, and increases sales-cycle complexity)

## 21. Signs the Person Is Not the Right Decision-Maker

- Junior title with no evidence of budget authority
- Role clearly execution-only (specialist, coordinator, assistant, associate)
- No visible connection to company leadership or strategic decisions

## 22. ICP Scoring Framework (0–100) — Deterministic

Exclusion criteria (Section 15) are checked first and override scoring. Any exclusion match → **Reject**, no score calculated.

The score represents **fit based on available evidence** — it is not, and must never be represented as, a confirmation of actual revenue or spend. A separate **Disclosure Status** field (Section D below) tracks whether real figures are actually known, and governs what the Skill is allowed to say in outreach.

#### Factor Weights

| Factor | Max Points |
|---|---|
| Revenue-Fit (Company Scale Signal) | 25 |
| Paid Acquisition Activity / Intensity Fit | 25 |
| Marketing/ecommerce maturity | 15 |
| Decision-maker involvement/contactability | 15 |
| Buying/trigger signal | 15 |
| DTC/business model fit | 5 |

---

#### A. Evidence-Tier Table (applies to Revenue-Fit and Paid Acquisition Activity/Intensity Fit only)

| Tier | Multiplier | Determined by |
|---|---|---|
| CONFIRMED | 100% | A primary source directly states the actual figure |
| STRONG EVIDENCE | 80% | 2+ fixed signal categories present |
| LIKELY | 40% | Exactly 1 fixed signal category present |
| UNKNOWN | 0% | 0 categories present |

**Corroboration rule (fixed, no exceptions):** Evidence tier is determined solely by counting how many distinct signal categories from the fixed list are present. Two or more present categories = STRONG EVIDENCE, regardless of how strong any individual category looks. STRONG EVIDENCE can never be upgraded to CONFIRMED by corroboration — CONFIRMED requires a primary-source statement, full stop.

**Revenue-Fit (Company Scale Signal):** Produces a revenue-fit *hypothesis*, not proof. Employee count, Shopify Plus tier, and fulfillment signals indicate company scale, which correlates with but does not confirm the $1M–$10M range. The Skill must never state a specific revenue figure or claim "confirmed $1M–$10M revenue" unless CONFIRMED tier is met via a primary source. At STRONG EVIDENCE or LIKELY tier, this must be described as "scale signals consistent with the target range," never as a revenue claim.

Signal categories:
1. Employee count (via LinkedIn company page or equivalent) falls within a band of roughly 5–75 employees
2. Shopify Plus tier detected (vs. standard Shopify)
3. Warehouse/fulfillment job postings or public references indicating physical operations at meaningful scale
4. 2 or more Paid Acquisition Activity/Intensity Fit signal categories (below) are independently present

If 0 categories present → UNKNOWN → **Revenue-Fit points = 0. No partial credit is permitted under any circumstance.**

**Paid Acquisition Activity / Intensity Fit:** Measures evidence of paid acquisition *activity*, not proof of a $15k+/month spend figure. CONFIRMED requires a primary source explicitly stating a spend figure ≥$15k/month — this will be rare. STRONG EVIDENCE and LIKELY describe activity/intensity only. The Skill must never state or imply a dollar spend amount unless CONFIRMED tier is met; at STRONG/LIKELY it may say "evidence of meaningful paid acquisition activity" or "evidence of paid acquisition activity," never a dollar figure.

Signal categories:
1. Meta Ad Library shows at least one active ad within the last 30 days
2. Google Search/Shopping ads visibly active for brand and/or category terms
3. A paid-media-specific job posting (media buyer, growth marketer, paid social specialist) posted within the last 90 days
4. The company is publicly named as a client in a paid-media agency's case study or client list

If 0 categories present → UNKNOWN → **Paid Acquisition Activity/Intensity points = 0.** This value must never be inferred, estimated, or assumed from unrelated signals — only from this fixed category list.

---

#### B. Checklist Factors

These do not use evidence tiers. Each sub-item is a binary presence/absence check with a fixed point value. No sub-item may be partially awarded.

**Marketing/Ecommerce Maturity (15 max):**
- 2 or more concurrent paid channels detected → 5 pts, else 0
- 2 or more retention/ecommerce tools detected from {Klaviyo, Recharge, Yotpo, Gorgias} → 5 pts, else 0 (1 tool = 0; threshold is fixed at 2)
- At least one of {GA4, GTM, Meta Pixel} detected on-site → 3 pts, else 0
- 2 or more distinct product categories/lines evident in the catalog → 2 pts, else 0

**Decision-Maker Involvement/Contactability (15 max) — LinkedIn-first:**
- Role check: Founder/Co-founder/CEO/Owner → 8 pts. VP/Head/Director of Marketing or Growth *with* at least one corroborating authority signal → 6 pts. Any other marketing-adjacent title with no corroborating authority signal → 3 pts. Execution-only title (Specialist/Coordinator/Assistant/Associate) → 0 pts.
- Public visibility: decision-maker has visible public activity (LinkedIn posts, press quotes) about the business → 4 pts, else 0
- Correct target decision-maker LinkedIn profile identified (name, current role, and current employer at the target company all confirmed) → 3 pts, else 0. Email availability is not required for this factor to score highly.

**Buying/Trigger Signal (15 max):**
A signal only counts as a verified trigger if it is (a) sourced with a specific URL/reference, and (b) does not require an implied comparison over time unless the Skill actually holds two dated observations proving the change. "Ad volume increase" is **not** a valid trigger unless the Skill has stored, dated historical data showing volume before and after — current ad presence alone is activity evidence (feeds Paid Acquisition Activity/Intensity Fit), not a trigger.

Valid trigger types (each needs a cited source): new product launch; store/brand expansion (new geography/marketplace); active marketing/growth hiring (job posting within 90 days); funding/growth announcement; visible funnel/offer change (new landing page, new promo structure); a new paid channel appearing where the Skill has a prior dated observation confirming its prior absence.

Scoring: 0 verified triggers → 0 pts. Exactly 1 verified, sourced trigger → 10 pts. 2 or more independent verified, sourced triggers → 15 pts.

**DTC/Business Model Fit (5 max):**
- Confirmed DTC/ecommerce model with owned acquisition funnel → 5 pts
- Anything else that did not already trigger exclusion under Section 15 → 0 pts

---

#### C. Score Bands

- 90–100 → **A** (Highest Priority)
- 75–89 → **B** (Good Prospect)
- 60–74 → **C** (Low Priority)
- Below 60 → **Reject**

#### D. Disclosure Status (separate field — does not affect score)

Every prospect record carries two additional tags, independent of the scoring math above:

- **Revenue Disclosure Status:** CONFIRMED / NOT CONFIRMED / UNKNOWN
- **Paid Spend Disclosure Status:** CONFIRMED / NOT CONFIRMED / UNKNOWN

"NOT CONFIRMED" means indirect evidence exists (feeding the score above) but no primary source states an actual figure. "UNKNOWN" means no evidence of either kind exists.

**Hard rule, non-negotiable regardless of score or tier:** the Skill may state a specific revenue or spend figure (e.g., "$5M brand," "$20k/month ad spend") only when the corresponding Disclosure Status is CONFIRMED. At NOT CONFIRMED or UNKNOWN — including for A-tier prospects — outreach and internal notes must use observable-fact language only: e.g., "running across Meta and Google," "hiring for a growth role," "recently expanded into [X]." No dollar figures, no "wasting $X" framing, no revenue-tier labels, ever, without a CONFIRMED source.

A-tier is reachable through STRONG EVIDENCE on both Revenue-Fit and Paid Acquisition Activity/Intensity Fit, combined with strong performance on the other four factors — a confirmed public revenue or spend figure is not required to reach A-tier. See worked examples below.

#### E. Enterprise Scale (clarification, not a ceiling)

$1M–$10M is the preferred revenue target, not a hard ceiling enforced by the scoring formula — there is no invented revenue cap. Companies that are clearly too large for Metrivio's founding-client motion are filtered out through the exclusion criteria (Section 15/20: enterprise-scale, multi-brand portfolio, or an already-sophisticated internal analytics function performing this work at scale), not through a revenue-based point penalty.

#### F. Worked Examples (fully reproducible from the rules above)

**A-Tier — Score 90 (boundary case, STRONG-evidence path to A, no confirmed figures)**
- Revenue-Fit: employee band + Shopify Plus = 2 categories → STRONG EVIDENCE → 25 × 0.80 = **20**
- Paid Acquisition Activity/Intensity: Meta ad active + Google ads active = 2 categories → STRONG EVIDENCE → 25 × 0.80 = **20**
- Maturity: 2+ channels (5) + Klaviyo & Recharge (5) + Pixel (3) + 2+ product lines (2) = **15**
- Decision-maker: Founder (8) + public LinkedIn presence (4) + correct target profile identified (3) = **15**
- Trigger: growth-role job posting (sourced, 60 days) + new Google channel confirmed vs. prior dated Meta-only observation = 2 verified = **15**
- Business model: DTC confirmed = **5**
- **Total: 20 + 20 + 15 + 15 + 15 + 5 = 90 → A**
- Disclosure Status: Revenue = NOT CONFIRMED, Spend = NOT CONFIRMED. Outreach must use observable-fact language only — no dollar figures despite A-tier classification.

**A-Tier — Score 95 (CONFIRMED path, for contrast)**
- Revenue-Fit: founder quoted stating "just over $4M" in a trade publication → CONFIRMED → **25**
- Paid Acquisition Activity/Intensity: Meta + Google active = STRONG EVIDENCE → **20**
- Maturity: **15** · Decision-maker: **15** · Trigger: **15** · Business model: **5**
- **Total: 25 + 20 + 15 + 15 + 15 + 5 = 95 → A**
- Disclosure Status: Revenue = CONFIRMED, Spend = NOT CONFIRMED. The revenue figure may be referenced internally if ever needed; the spend figure still may not be stated.

**B-Tier — Score 83**
- Revenue-Fit: employee band + Shopify Plus = 2 categories → STRONG EVIDENCE → **20**
- Paid Acquisition Activity/Intensity: Meta ad active + paid-media job posting = 2 categories → STRONG EVIDENCE → **20**
- Maturity: 2+ channels (5) + Klaviyo & Gorgias (5) + GA4 (3) + 2+ product lines (2) = **15**
- Decision-maker: Director with corroborating authority signal (6) + public visibility (4) + correct target profile identified (3) = **13**
- Trigger: 1 verified (growth-role job posting, 60 days) = **10**
- Business model: DTC confirmed = **5**
- **Total: 20 + 20 + 15 + 13 + 10 + 5 = 83 → B**

**C-Tier — Score 63**
- Revenue-Fit: Shopify Plus only = 1 category → LIKELY → 25 × 0.40 = **10**
- Paid Acquisition Activity/Intensity: Meta ad active + Google ads active = 2 categories → STRONG EVIDENCE → **20**
- Maturity: 2+ channels (5) + 1 retention tool, below threshold (0) + GA4 (3) + single product line (0) = **8**
- Decision-maker: Director, no corroborating authority signal (3) + public visibility (4) + correct target profile identified (3) = **10**
- Trigger: 1 verified = **10**
- Business model: DTC confirmed = **5**
- **Total: 10 + 20 + 8 + 10 + 10 + 5 = 63 → C**

**Reject — Exclusion Override**
No paid acquisition activity detected on any channel; only contactable person is listed as "Customer Support." Meets Section 15 exclusion criteria (no paid acquisition activity, no plausible decision-maker). **Score is not calculated. Reject (exclusion).**

**Reject — Low Score, No Exclusion Triggered**
- Revenue-Fit: 0 categories present → UNKNOWN → **0** (hard rule, no partial credit)
- Paid Acquisition Activity/Intensity: 1 category (single Meta ad detected) → LIKELY → 25 × 0.40 = **10**
- Maturity: 1 channel only (0) + 1 retention tool, below threshold (0) + GA4 detected (3) + single product line (0) = **3**
- Decision-maker: "Marketing Manager," no corroborating authority signal (3) + no public visibility (0) + correct target profile identified (3) = **6**
- Trigger: 0 verified signals = **0**
- Business model: DTC confirmed = **5**
- **Total: 0 + 10 + 3 + 6 + 0 + 5 = 24 → Reject (score)**

### Scoring Rule for the Skill

Every score the Skill outputs must be accompanied by a short written justification listing which factors contributed points, at what evidence tier, and the Disclosure Status for revenue and spend — never output a bare number.
